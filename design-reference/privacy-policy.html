Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

VOIDINK Studios ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website voidinkstudios.netlify.app and use our creative services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you contact us, request a quote, or place an order, we may collect:

  • Full name and email address
  • Company or brand name (if applicable)
  • Country and timezone
  • Budget range and project deadlines
  • Project descriptions and creative briefs
  • Payment information (processed securely via third-party gateways)

1.2 Artwork & Creative Assets

For commission work, you may share reference images, character descriptions, brand guidelines, or existing assets. We treat all submitted materials as confidential.

1.3 Automatically Collected Data

We use basic analytics tools that may collect:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited and time spent on site
  • Referring website or search terms
  • Device type and screen resolution

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide services: Communicating about your project, delivering files, and providing support.
  • To process payments: Invoicing and payment collection via secure gateways (Stripe, PayPal, Wise, or Payoneer).
  • To improve our services: Understanding which services are most popular and optimizing our workflow.
  • To send updates: Project status updates, delivery notifications, and occasional service announcements (only with consent).
  • To showcase work: With your explicit permission, we may display completed projects in our portfolio.

3. Artwork Confidentiality & NSFW Content

VOIDINK Studios specializes in a wide range of creative services, including personal and sensitive artwork (e.g., character designs, furry art, NSFW commissions). We guarantee:

  • Complete confidentiality: Your identity, project details, and artwork are never shared without written consent.
  • Secure file storage: All project files are stored on encrypted cloud storage with restricted access.
  • NDA available: We can sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement upon request for sensitive commercial projects.
  • No unauthorized use: We do not use your commissioned artwork for AI training datasets or sell it to third parties.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses minimal cookies for functionality and basic analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work optimally.

5. Third-Party Services

We may share limited data with trusted third parties solely for operational purposes:

  • Payment processors (Stripe, PayPal, Wise, Payoneer) — for secure transactions
  • Email services — for project communication and delivery
  • Cloud storage — for file backup and transfer
  • Analytics — for website performance insights (anonymized data only)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

6. Data Retention

We retain your project files and communication history for 12 months after project completion to handle any revision requests or disputes. After this period, files are permanently deleted unless you request earlier removal or grant permission for portfolio use.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at voidinkstudios@gmail.com.

8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted file transfers, password-protected delivery links, and secure payment processing. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
